About

What is
IGOLD?

IGOLD is a free, interactive guide to solat — built for anyone learning to pray, or returning to prayer, and for the teachers and families who guide them.

Rather than a wall of text, every step is demonstrated on video, recited aloud, and explained in plain language. Choose your school of law, follow along at your own pace, and revise whenever you need to.
